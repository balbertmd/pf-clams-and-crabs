Good to know

Questions from the dock

The stuff folks ask us most. Anything else, just call or text - we'd rather talk to you.

Can I buy clams and crabs straight from you?

You bet. Paul sells straight from the dock - no middleman, no markup. Call or text (609) 305-3836 to reserve today's catch for pickup.

How much are they?

Prices move with the season and the tides, so we don't post a set number. Call or text for today's price - you'll always get the fresh number.

Are the blue crabs running right now?

Blue crabs are seasonal - Paul runs his own pots, so they're in when they're running. Give us a shout to see what's in today.

Where and when do I pick up?

Right at the dock: 1044 W Rio Grande Ave, Wildwood, NJ. Hours are seasonal and we set them by the day, so call ahead before you make the trip.

Do you ship or deliver?

Not this year - it's local dock pickup only for 2026, cash or card. Shipping may come down the road.

What clams do you have?

Farm-raised hard clams - littlenecks for the raw bar, topnecks for the pot - year-round. Live Jersey blue crabs when they're in season.
